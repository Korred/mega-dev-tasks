Decision: merge only with evidence of the same cause

The explorer applies the supplied tenant-based model. Its cases show why a subject-only rule fails: the same subject can separate by channel or tenant, and different subjects can share a cause. A tenant missing from the directory has no established cause: keep the report separate and investigate. Two missing causes are not proof of a duplicate. Also keep reports separate when their bodies contradict the model or do not contain enough evidence, as the notes above illustrate.

What changes for the desk

Keep separate parser and form-hint tickets plus one investigation ticket for each unresolved report, retaining report IDs, channel, tenant and observed input/output. Ask for reproduction details where missing. Verify each fix on the affected path before closing its ticket; closing one must not close the other.

This replaces the instruction to merge when unsure. It costs more reading up front, but prevents one queued fix from hiding a different problem.
